Questions

Good to know.

How often can I get deliveries?

Weekly or monthly, for a term of 1, 3, 6, 12 or 18 months. A weekly plan creates one delivery every seven days, so a one-month weekly plan has four or five deliveries depending on your start date.

Can I skip a delivery?

Yes. In the dashboard, skip the next delivery and the rest of your schedule stays as it was.

Can I pause and resume?

Yes. Pausing holds your schedule. When you resume, remaining dates are recalculated from the next available date using the same rules as the builder.

How do I cancel?

Cancel from the dashboard after a confirmation step. In this demo, cancelling only updates your saved data.

When is shipping free?

Orders with a subtotal of ₹999 or more, measured after any coupon discount, ship free. Otherwise a flat demo shipping fee applies.

How fresh is the coffee?

This demo doesn’t make freshness claims. A real store would show roast dates on every bag.

What grind options are there?

Whole bean, espresso, moka pot, filter and French press. Grind is separate from roast level, which describes how dark the beans are.

How should I brew loose-leaf tea?

Each tea has its own brew guide on its product page, with water temperature and steeping time.

Can I send a gift subscription?

Yes, in step 3. Gift delivery and recipient messages are simulated in this demo.

What are the demo’s limits?

No payment, courier, email or real account exists. Data is saved in this browser and can be cleared at any time.
