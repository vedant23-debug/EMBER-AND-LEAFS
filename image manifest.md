# EMBER & LEAF — image manifest

## What you see right now
Every image slot already shows a real photo. If a file is missing from your `images` folder, the site automatically shows a stand-in photo from Unsplash (the same ones your original site used). The stand-ins are generic coffee and tea photos shared across products, so several products look alike until you replace them. If a stand-in ever fails to load, you see a "Photo coming soon" box instead.

## How to replace a stand-in with a permanent local photo
1. Click the stand-in link in the table, or search the phrase on Unsplash or Pexels (free licences; check each photo page).
2. Download the photo and rename it to EXACTLY the name in the first column.
3. Put it in that folder next to `index.html` (for example `images/coffee/`). Refresh. Your file now takes priority automatically.

Best sizes: about 800 px wide, portrait (4:5) for products; 1400 px wide for hero, story and plans.

Your own photos of your real packaging are best, because stock photos only show the type of product, not what you actually sell.
